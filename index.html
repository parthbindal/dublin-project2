    How it works

    1. An AI model running inside your browser spots objects in the camera feed.
    2. Binsight matches each object to California sorting rules.
    3. You get the bin, the reason and a tip, read out loud if voice is on.

    Everything runs on your device. Your camera feed and photos never leave it.

    Guidance follows general California rules. Your city may differ, so check your waste hauler's guide.
